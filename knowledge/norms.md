# Commerce KPI Norms (Typical Ranges)

- Conversion rate: 1% to 6%
- Add-to-cart rate: 5% to 15%
- Bounce rate: 30% to 60%
- ROAS: 2x to 6x
- AOV: varies by sector; compare shop-to-shop

Directional guidance
- Higher conversion_rate, add_to_cart_rate, roas are better.
- Lower bounce_rate and cpc are better.
- Use sector context for AOV (apparel vs furniture).
