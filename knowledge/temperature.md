Title: Thermal Comfort — Optimal Temperatures for Work

Productive office environments balance comfort and alertness. Most occupants feel comfortable in a moderate temperature band. Too warm can reduce alertness; too cool can increase distraction and complaints.

Guidance
- Aim for a stable, moderate temperature range suitable for sedentary work.
- Avoid rapid swings; stability matters as much as the absolute setpoint.
- Consider local factors: sun exposure, equipment heat, and clothing norms.

Operational responses
- Tune zone setpoints and deadbands to reduce overshoot.
- Verify sensor placement and calibration.
- Address hot/cold spots with balancing and diffuser adjustments.
