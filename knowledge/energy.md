Title: Energy Use — Interpreting Consumption and Cost

Energy consumption reflects how systems meet comfort and ventilation needs. Higher use can stem from longer runtimes, higher outdoor air fractions, or inefficient equipment. Cost awareness encourages targeted efficiency.

Understanding cost
- Electricity is typically billed per kilowatt‑hour. Prices vary by region and contract; use a representative average when estimating cost impact.
- Focus on relative changes over time and between operational modes rather than exact pennies.

Operational responses
- Align ventilation and temperature schedules with actual occupancy.
- Maintain heat recovery and economizers to temper fresh air efficiently.
- Target persistent baseloads and after‑hours use for reduction.
